How the loop worked

Each iteration produced two commits: one substantive change, and a tiny follow-up backfilling the iteration's SHA into IDEAS.md's "Done" section. The end-of-turn summary you saw after each iteration was always preceded by a hard validation pass that I didn't ship if anything regressed. That's why score never dropped between commits — every commit was either neutral or an improvement.

I started by pulling pressure-tested skills from the Owl-Listener/designer-skills repo (1094★) and seeding IDEAS.md with the issues from our chat plus what I noticed reading the code. After working through the obvious items I switched to deliberate audit passes, looking for bugs the backlog didn't surface. That's when most of the real bugs showed up.

Things I noticed about the workflow

  • Verify-before-fix paid off. Three iters (6, 18, 19) turned out to be either non-bugs or fixes-of-the-wrong-thing. Measuring first kept the backlog honest.
  • Audit-driven work outperformed backlog-driven near the tail. The first ~25 iters drained the IDEAS.md list. The next 10 found 5 real bugs via code audit — none of which were in the original backlog.
  • HMR transient errors are noise. Multiple times the console showed "X is not defined" errors during my edit window. Always cleared after a fresh reload. The validation rule of "no NEW console errors after fresh reload" held; mid-edit ghosts were always real but transient.
  • The hidden-tab RAF throttle limited visual verification. The MCP Chrome tab kept drifting to visibilityState: "hidden", which paused requestAnimationFrame. The spin animation wouldn't complete in that state, and the IntersectionObserver wouldn't fire. Worked around it for state-end testing by preloading URL hashes (e.g. #s=...pick=belle-isle) to land directly in the post-spin state without needing to drive the animation.
  • "App.tsx is a giant component" wasn't really fixable. Even after two sub-component extractions (MapPane, WheelPane), react-doctor still flags App at the no-giant-component threshold. App is the orchestration layer that owns state and callbacks; further extraction would have meant moving spin/rotateTo logic into custom hooks, which would have touched the off-limits spin animation code. Stopped.

What's left in IDEAS.md

  • Controls drawer/sheet UI on mobile (real redesign — needs design judgment, not autonomous work).
  • Two react-doctor warnings that are false positives: rerender-state-only-in-handlers on loaded in RichmondMap and shouldLoad in DeferredMap. Both states ARE read in conditional render paths, but the static analysis misses that. Documented; not "fixable" without uglier patterns.
  • prefer-useReducer on App with 6 useState. Could fold animation state into a second reducer but that's invasive and a stylistic call.
  • Color contrast on dim POI strokes. Below WCAG 3:1 but those dots are intentionally dim (ineligible). Design intent > metric.
