Felt more than heard

Every cue is synthesized in the page — no files, nothing loaded — and sits at whisper level under a shared master volume. High, short tones for fingertip contact; a single low thump reserved for the one moment that matters, the reel landing. The ratchet inherits the reel's timing — a four-second throw whose flips ease from 30 ms out to 320 ms — and its pitch falls as it slows.
